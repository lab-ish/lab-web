2026年10月11日〜10月15日にShanghai, Chinaで開催される[ACM Ubicomp](https://www.ubicomp.org/ubicomp-iswc-2026/)併催[Intelligent Soft Wearables Workshop](https://softwearables.github.io/)において、M2 井戸、M1 田中、石田准教授で1件の発表を行います。

## 論文情報

- Satoshi Ido*, Keiji Tanaka*, Riku Maeda*, Ryo Takahashi, Akihito Noda, and Shigemi Ishida, "Collisionless Multi-Tag NFC Sensing for Indoor Activity Recognition Using Meander E-Textiles," Companion of the 2026 ACM International Joint Conference on Pervasive and Ubiquitous Computing (Ubicomp Companion '26), Oct 2026.
  - *: These authors contributed equally to this research
